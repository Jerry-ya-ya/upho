Acoustic & optical phonon modes

Longitudinal vibrations of a 1D chain with two atoms per unit cell and identical springs.

Phonon dispersion

The acoustic branch reaches zero frequency at Γ. The optical branch remains at a finite frequency.

Frequencies are in units of √(K/m), where K is the spring constant.
